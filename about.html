About Afri Geoinformation Engineering PLC

Established in 2005 G.C. (1997 E.C.) - Over 20 Years of Geospatial and Transport Engineering Excellence.

Who We Are

Afri Geoinformation Engineering PLC is an Ethiopian Geographic Information System (GIS), Information Technology (GIS/IT), and Engineering consulting firm founded in 2005 G.C. (1997 E.C.) by GIS experts, Civil Engineers, Computer Science professionals, Surveyors, and Cartographers.

Vision:To be the leading Geospatial and Socioeconomic Company working in the areas of GIS, Remote Sensing, and Socioeconomic Studies across the globe.

Mission:Providing quality services in GIS, Remote Sensing, and Socioeconomic Studies while striving for client satisfaction.

Our Expertise

  • Geographic Information Systems (GIS)
  • Remote Sensing
  • Transportation Engineering
  • Topographic Surveying
  • Socioeconomic Studies
  • Geospatial Data Management
  • Road and Infrastructure Planning

Current and Recent Flagship Engagements

Addis Ababa Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) Corridor Projects

Conducting topographic surveys, traffic counts, Origin-Destination (OD) surveys, boarding and alighting surveys, speed surveys, and Right-of-Way (RoW) spatial analysis in joint venture with DOHWA-PYUNGHWA Engineering Co., Ltd and Gondwana Engineering PLC for the Addis Ababa City Roads Authority.

Strengthening GIS Team within Ethiopian Roads Authority

Serving as Main Consultant to develop an Enterprise Geodatabase, design the Ethiopian Road Information System, train staff across directorates, and guide IT infrastructure implementation.
