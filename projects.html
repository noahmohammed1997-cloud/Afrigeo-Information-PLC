Our Project Experience

Afri Geoinformation Engineering PLC has participated in various geospatial and engineering consultancy engagements. Our work focuses on providing technical solutions that support infrastructure development and informed decision-making.

1. Addis Ababa Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) Projects

AfriGeo is involved in the Addis Ababa Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) corridor projects, supporting transportation planning and infrastructure development.

Project Activities

  • Topographic surveys
  • Traffic volume counts
  • Origin-Destination (OD) surveys
  • Boarding and alighting surveys
  • Speed surveys
  • Right-of-Way (RoW) spatial analysis
  • BRT corridor mapping and feasibility studies

2. Ethiopian Roads Authority (ERA) GIS Project

AfriGeo has served as the main consultant in strengthening the GIS team within the Ethiopian Roads Authority (ERA).

Project Activities

  • Enterprise Geodatabase development
  • Ethiopian Road Information System design
  • GIS capacity building and staff training
  • IT infrastructure implementation guidance
  • Geospatial data management

3. Topographic Surveying and Mapping

Our surveying and mapping activities support infrastructure planning, engineering design, and spatial information development.

  • Topographic data collection
  • Land surveying
  • Digital map production
  • Spatial data analysis

4. Transportation Planning and Traffic Studies

We undertake transportation studies and traffic analysis to support effective transportation planning and infrastructure development.

  • Traffic analysis
  • Transportation corridor studies
  • Traffic data collection
  • Road network planning
